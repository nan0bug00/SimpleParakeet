NVIDIA Parakeet model weights
=============================

SimpleParakeet downloads one selected Sherpa ONNX export on first launch. The
model is not bundled in the release archive.

English model:
  NVIDIA Parakeet TDT-CTC 110M
  https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m

Multilingual model:
  NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3
  https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3

Both model cards identify the weights as Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(CC BY 4.0). The full license text is in LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt.

The ONNX exports are downloaded from the official sherpa-onnx model releases:
  https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/asr-models
